Travel requests

Request approval for business travel.

Describe the trip and what it will cost. The request is checked against your organisation's travel policy before it reaches your approver, so you see anything that needs fixing first.

Submit a travel request Review requests waiting for you — sign-in required Submit expenses after a trip
How it works

From request to decision

  1. You enter the trip: where, when, why, and the fares and rates you were quoted.
  2. The travel policy checks it and names any rule it breaks, with what to change.
  3. Your approver reviews the request and approves or declines it.
  4. After the trip, you submit receipts for reimbursement.
